How to reach IIIT-H

How to make your way to the campus:


Landmarks

1. Gachibowli Stadium
2. Indian School of Business
3. TCS Synergy park ODC-6
4. DLF Cyber City, Hyderabad
5. Gachibowli Police Station


Airport - Rajiv Gandhi International Airport

Approximately 22 km from IIIT-H

Pushpak Electric Bus directly from Airport to Gachibowli Circle and vice versa (3.5Km from Campus)
Fare: Around 300/- to 350/- per person
For timings visit: Pushpak Bus Timings

Direct cab from airport to campus
Approximate fare: 800/- to 1000/- for a 4-seater cab and 1300/- to 1800/- for a 6-seater cab (subject to change based on time and availability)


Railway Stations

Direct Options:

Secunderabad (Approx 25 km to IIIT-H)
Direct Auto to IIIT - Around ₹300/- to ₹400/-
Direct Cab (4-Seater) to IIIT - Around ₹350/- to ₹450/-
Direct Cab (6-Seater) to IIIT - Around ₹400/-

Nampally (Approx 18 km to IIIT-H)
Direct Auto to IIIT - Approx ₹350/-
Direct Cab (4-Seater) to IIIT - Approx ₹350/-
Direct Cab (6-Seater) to IIIT - Approx ₹500/-

Kacheguda (Approx 23 km to IIIT-H)
Direct Auto to IIIT - Approx ₹350/- to ₹400/-
Direct Cab (4-seater) to IIIT - Approx ₹270/- to ₹380/-
Direct Cab (6-Seater) to IIIT - Approx ₹550/-

Cherlapally (Approx 38 km to IIIT-H)
Direct Cab (4-Seater) to IIIT - Approx ₹550/- to ₹650/-
Direct Cab (6-Seater) to IIIT - Approx ₹1050/-


Metro Option:

Metro services are available from:
• Secunderabad Metro Station
• Nampally Metro Station
to Raidurg Metro Station (6km from campus)
Please check Metro routes online for current schedules and routes.


MMTS Train Option:

From all the above railway stations, you can reach Lingampally station by MMTS (local train).
Lingampally is the closest MMTS station to IIIT (approx 7km)
Auto fare from Lingampally - Approx ₹120/-


Cabs


Uber Cabs [Download mobile app from Playstore]

Ola Cabs [Download mobile app from Playstore]

Rapido [Download mobile app from Playstore]


Buses are also readily available from all over Hyderabad to IIIT-H at low fares.
(For newcomers to Hyderabad -- please do get it confirmed before boarding if the bus follows the route to IIIT.)
