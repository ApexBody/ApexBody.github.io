Letter from Director

Dear Incoming Students,

Welcome to IIIT Hyderabad!


You are joining us at an exciting time, when advances in computing, artificial intelligence, and technology are transforming the world around us. As a student at IIIT Hyderabad, you will have the opportunity not only to learn about these changes, but also to help shape them.

The years ahead will be filled with challenges, discoveries, and opportunities for growth. I hope you will make the most of everything IIIT Hyderabad has to offer-whether through coursework, research, innovation or the rich campus life beyond the classroom.

One of the defining strengths of IIIT Hyderabad is its strong research orientation. We encourage students at every level to explore ideas deeply, ask difficult questions, and engage with problems that matter. Some of your richest learning experiences will come from stepping beyond what is required and pursuing what genuinely interests and excites you.

As you build technical expertise, I also encourage you to think about the broader impact of your work. Technology has its greatest value when it serves people and contributes positively to society. The world needs innovators who combine knowledge with responsibility, imagination with integrity.

You are now part of a community that has been shaped by generations of students, faculty, researchers, and alumni who have pushed boundaries and created lasting impact. I hope you will embrace the opportunities before you, forge lifelong friendships, and make your own unique contribution to this legacy.

Last but not the least, we expect our students to be ethical, conscientious, honest and compassionate in all that they do. At IIIT Hyderabad we believe that our students should embody the three Cs Competence, Creativity, and Care. As you pursue excellence and achieve your goals, do so with integrity and a strong sense of responsibility, mindful of the impact your actions have on humanity and the environment. You are citizens of this country and the world. Building a sustainable and liveable future for the next generation is a responsibility we all share.

The journey ahead is uniquely yours - all the best!


Prof. Sandeep Shukla

Director

IIIT Hyderabad
